# Changelog

## 1.2.0

Added
- Data fields: next sunrise or sunset, weekly intensity minutes, altitude.
- Low-battery warning: the Battery field turns to the accent color at 15% or below.
- German, French and Spanish translations.
- 12-hour time drops the leading zero and shows a small A or P.
- Orbit tracker: starfield.
- Targeting: scrolling counters above the trench, panel lines on the floor and walls, outer brackets that close in on the reticle with step progress.
- Options: Slanted digits, CRT glow (glow under the time and main outlines, plus scanlines).

Changed
- Always-on mode no longer reads Body Battery, stress, weather or sun times; they are read fresh on wake.
- Always-on picture shifts sideways and vertically every minute (25 positions).
- Settings are read once and again only when they change.
- Floors shows "--" on watches without a barometer.

Removed
- Tap-debug overlay.

## 1.1.0

Added
- Seconds data field.
- Show seconds option: small seconds next to the time.

## 1.0.0

First release: Orbit Tracker, Targeting and Radar styles, custom colors and data fields, weather, moon phase, press-and-hold widgets, English and Polish.
